# Naming Direction

## Recommendation

Use **Timebox Mark** if the product pivots from a Pomodoro timer into the daily planning sheet shown in the design references.

Keep **Focus Mark** if the app remains primarily a focus timer with Live Activity support.

## Why Timebox Mark

- It describes the real product loop: plan the day, mark the active time box, then review the result.
- It gives the project a broader future than Pomodoro.
- It still fits the existing black-and-white visual identity and app icon.
- It avoids naming the app after an implementation detail such as MethodChannel or Pigeon.

## Alternatives

- **Daybox**: short and memorable, but less explicit.
- **Time Grid**: clear, but sounds generic.
- **Focus Ledger**: polished, but more reflective than action-oriented.
- **Today Box**: friendly, but less professional.

## Current Decision

Do not rename the bundle yet. The app currently ships as **Focus Mark**. Rename after the timeboxing planner becomes the first screen, because changing the app name before the core flow exists can make the portfolio story feel ahead of the product.
