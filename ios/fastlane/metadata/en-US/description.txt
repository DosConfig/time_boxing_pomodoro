Timebox Mark helps you plan today with less effort.

Capture what is on your mind, choose the top priorities, place work into 30-minute time boxes, and run the current block from Focus. Local alerts and Live Activity keep the active block visible, while calendar export lets you send selected blocks to your calendar when you choose.

Key features:
- Brain dump capture
- Top three daily priorities
- 30-minute timebox board
- Focus timer synced to the current block
- Local completion alerts
- iOS Live Activity support
- Apple Calendar export
- Google Calendar export
- Firebase account sync

Timebox Mark is designed for simple daily planning, not heavy project management.
