Timebox Mark helps you plan today with less effort.

Capture what is on your mind, choose the top priorities, place work into 30-minute time boxes, and run the current block from Focus. Local alerts and Live Activity keep the active block visible while your signed-in daily plan stays available across app launches.

Key features:
- Brain dump capture
- Top three daily priorities
- 30-minute timebox board
- Focus timer synced to the current block
- Local completion alerts
- iOS Live Activity support
- Firebase account sync

Timebox Mark is designed for simple daily planning, not heavy project management.
