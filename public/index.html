Timebox Mark

A focused timeboxing app for turning daily priorities into 30-minute blocks, Focus sessions, local alerts, and Live Activity.

Timeboxing

Capture what is on your mind, choose the top priorities, and place work into fixed 30-minute slots for the day.

Focus

The Focus screen follows the current time box so the active timer, Live Activity, and local notifications share one time source.

Continuity

Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your daily plan available when you reopen the app.
